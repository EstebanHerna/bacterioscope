## Use rules

- A source that validates geometry is not evidence of clinical measurement performance.
- The constructed SVG exercises contain no organism identity, antibiotic, S/I/R label, phenotype, or patient data.
- Every exercise and rule remains unavailable to the product until a qualified reviewer checks the image/task, wording, tolerance, and intended use. The reviewer must be named in the manifest; do not substitute an AI agent for that review.
- CLSI's official M100 page lists the 36th edition published in 2026. BacterioScope currently documents Ed33 (2023); this content pack does not update or reuse its breakpoint data.
- Do not copy CLSI/EUCAST tables into prompts, source code, exercises, or demos based on this inventory.
